The theoretical analysis rigorously extends prior work to non-diagonal matrices and establishes new expressivity results for LRNNs with extended eigenvalue ranges. The proposed modification is simple, well-motivated, and validated across synthetic and realistic tasks. However, the work has notable limitations: (1) the modification breaks compatibility with log-space computation in popular variants (Mamba2, GLA), severely limiting practical adoption; (2) large-scale LM improvements are modest and inconsistent, suggesting the expressivity gains may not translate to general downstream tasks; (3) theoretical results require exponential resources in worst case.

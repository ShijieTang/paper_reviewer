While the application is novel and experiments are comprehensive, the work suffers from fundamental soundness issues and incomplete evaluation. Most critically, using the model's own predictions as the attack target creates circular logic that conflates 'deviating from predictions' with 'fooling the model'—this is a weaker and conceptually different objective than traditional adversarial attacks against ground truth.

      "summary_comment": "This paper presents a solid contribution to understanding and exploiting symmetries in neural networks for discovering linear mode connectivity. The unified framework capturing four symmetry classes is conceptually clean and well-motivated, subsuming prior permutation-based approaches. The empirical results—achieving zero-loss linear interpolation paths between independently trained Transformers—are striking and represent a meaningful advance over prior work. The methods are technically sound, the evaluation is reasonably comprehensive, and the presentation is clear. However, the work has notable limitations: (1) evaluation is restricted to small-scale models due to computational constraints, limiting claims about practical applicability to modern large models; (2) some results (e.g., BookCorpus) remain unexplained, suggesting the method has limitations not fully characterized; (3) the framework requires architectural reparameterizations (LayerNorm reformulation, multiplying out attention dependencies) that complicate the interpretation; (4) soft permutations introduce fundamental trade-offs left unresolved. Despite these limitations, the paper makes a clear conceptual advance in understanding symmetries and demonstrates compelling empirical results on the datasets explored. The work is suitable for publication at a top-tier venue, with the understanding that larger-scale validation would strengthen the claims. The gap analysis and ablations are particularly valuable for practitioners. The main recommendation for acceptance is tempered by the recognition that the scope is somewhat bounded and some fundamental questions about generality and scalability remain open."

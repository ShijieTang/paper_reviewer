However, the work has three significant limitations that prevent it from being a strong accept: (1) the lack of comparison with heuristic-based dense LTL rewards [38], which is the main competing approach for addressing sparsity, limits claims of superiority; (2) all experiments are in simulation on relatively simple tasks with no real-world validation; (3) the theoretical analysis does not quantify how the soft labeling approximation affects satisfaction probability guarantees relative to discrete LTL rewards.
